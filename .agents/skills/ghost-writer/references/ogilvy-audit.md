# Writing Quality Pass

Apply after drafting, before the style-pattern pass. This generic adaptation of the original ghost workflow uses plain-language editing principles associated with David Ogilvy and George Orwell, without imposing a particular author's voice or fixed length limits.

## Fix before delivery

- **Grounding:** Check names, numbers, quotations, promises, and personal claims against the task evidence. Do not turn an external benchmark into a local result. Verify, qualify, omit, or ask about unsupported claims.
- **Meaning:** Preserve the intended message, qualifications, and audience. A more confident sentence is not better if it becomes false.
- **Purpose:** Make the action clear when action is the purpose. A simple answer does not need an invented call to action.
- **Clarity:** Remove ambiguity, unnecessary repetition, and jargon that obscures meaning. Keep technical terms the audience needs.

## Improve where appropriate

- Put the point where this register needs it; narrative writing may legitimately build toward it.
- Cut words that do no work, but retain warmth, rhythm, and nuance supported by the profile.
- Prefer concrete actors and mechanisms to empty abstractions. Do not invent numbers for specificity.
- Check paragraph shape, sentence variation, and length against the brief and matching samples, not a universal word count.
- Read the draft as speech in its intended register. Fix stiffness without forcing casualness on formal writing.
- For consequential communications, suggest human review when useful. Do not claim the draft was sent or arrange a delay without authorization.

If a generic rule damages meaning or a confirmed style preference, keep the deliberate choice. Factual integrity takes precedence over style.

## Audit-only output

Give a one-line assessment, followed by findings ordered by importance:

- **[Issue]** "Exact text" → concrete fix and brief reason.

End with what works and should survive editing. Omit empty categories. Keep the audit proportionate to the text. During drafting, apply the pass silently unless the user requests the audit.
